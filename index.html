Smallest by area, not by population

In 2023, ACT had 466,800 residents; NT had 252,500. ACT's larger population helps explain higher raw counts: 120,222 adults versus 65,641; 68,200 children versus 40,800. NT's adult participation rate was higher: 32.2% versus ACT's 30.8%. For children, ACT led 77.3% to 71.0%. The maps cannot explain rate differences.

Which activities lead?

In 2022-23 adult tables, ACT's top sport-related activity SA's leading sport was running/athletics (81,000). NT's leading sport was swimming (47,000). Swimming led the children's tables in both: 34,000 in ACT and 20,000 in NT. These are separate activity estimates, not components of the map totals. Treat small child estimates cautiously.

Reading the chart

Right means growth; left means decline. Circle area shows participants for the selected year, region and age group. Paired rows compare male (teal) and female (orange) participants. What these figures measure

Change = (selected-year count ÷ 2022 count − 1)×100. This measures relative growth, not participation rates or percentage points. Small baselines can produce large changes. 09 / FACILITY MEMBERSHIP

Who uses a local aquatic centre?

PARC—the Peninsula Aquatic Recreation Centre—is Frankston’s local aquatic and leisure centre, with pools, fitness, and learn-to-swim programs for children and adults.

These charts show membership types and one member-group profile. They describe enrolment, not every visitor or visit frequency. PARC recorded 708,716 member attendances in 2024–25, about 1.1% more than the previous year. Attendance counts visits; they are not unique people.

Swimming skills matter

PARC’s lessons teach swimming and water-safety skills. Nationally, 357 people drowned during 2024–25, 27% above average for the past ten years. That figure shows why water-safety education matters; it does not measure PARC’s local impact.

Membership choices

How to read this waffle Each of its 100 squares approximates one point. Shares come from reported member counts for each year. Square totals are rounded, so they are visual estimates. Membership type is not the same as visit frequency.

Gender of Full Access members

Read this profile within its scope The 40%, 59% and 1% shares cover Full Access members only, not every PARC member. They do not measure inclusion or affordability. Earlier reports use different scopes or unreconciled totals, so this chart shows the complete 2024–25 breakdown.

10 / FACILITY MIX

Record counts and area tell different stories.

Cricket pitches form 55.5% of all records. They contribute just 6.6% of measured area. Cricket/football features reverse that pattern: 13% of records, but 51.6% of area.

The area leaders

Two categories account for most recorded area

51.6%Cricket/Football

18.4%Soccer

Together, they represent about 70% of area, from 27% of mapped records. Combined fields cover 296,541 square metres.

Why the count and area differ

The mapped unit changes the comparison

Cricket pitches are the most frequent feature: 81 records, or 55.5% of the total. Yet they make up only 6.6% of recorded area.

Combined fields average about 15,600 m² each; cricket-pitch records average about 470 m². This may reflect whole shared ovals versus pitch features. The source does not confirm its mapping intent; overlapping features mean these are not exclusive parcels.

Read this as representation
