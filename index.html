07 / FOOTBALL PARTICIPATION

Where has football participation grown since 2022?

Fourth on home soil

The Matildas finished fourth at the 2023 Women’s World Cup, their best finish at that tournament. These participation figures cannot establish a World Cup effect.

Matildas: 2023 Year in Review

Compare each region with its own 2022 baseline. Select an age group and comparison year.

Reading the chart

Right means growth; left means decline. Circle area shows participants for the selected year, region and age group. Paired rows compare male (teal) and female (orange) participants. Both use the same circle-size and growth scales. Growth scales stay fixed across years but change between ages. Hover for counts and source pages.

What these figures measure

Change = (selected-year count ÷ 2022 count − 1)×100. This measures relative growth, not participation rates or percentage points. Small baselines can produce large changes. NSW and Northern NSW remain separate regions. Only published male and female counts are included; unspecified gender is excluded. Age categories follow the source reports.

How the funding records are used

The CSV combines original rows from three workbooks. Repeated-looking records remain because no project identifier resolves duplicates. Program Round standardises the original Program Name column. Dates use day/month/year; other source values are preserved.

Charts calculate counts, shares and ranks from source rows. Council spelling variants are matched inside the map specification. Purpose shares are calculated separately for each approval year. Different purpose labels are not treated as identical.
