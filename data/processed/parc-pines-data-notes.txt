PARC annual-report transcriptions, 2022-23 to 2024-25

Prepared 2026-10-22 for the FIT3179 visualisation project.
Source: Peninsula Leisure annual reports. These CSVs are manually transcribed
from the official PDFs and visually checked. They are not official CSV releases.
This cleaned extract retains only the membership datasets used in the webpage.

FILES
parc-membership-types.csv: 9 membership-type rows, with counts, reported shares
  where available, and separately calculated shares (members / total * 100).
parc-membership-demographics.csv: 8 gender rows with population scopes and flags.

FIELD CONVENTIONS
UTF-8 comma-separated files, one header row, numeric values without separators.
financial_year identifies the annual report, not a calendar year.
Percentage columns use 0-100 units (58 means 58%, not 0.58).
Blank cells mean not reported/not applicable, never zero. Missing years/categories
are not filled with zeros or estimated. Counts are not inferred from percentages.
source_printed_page is the page number printed on the report.
source_pdf_page is the 1-based page index in a PDF viewer; append #page=N to URL.
All rows carry their official source URL, page numbers and extraction method.
Exact membership snapshot dates are not specified in the membership panels.

VALIDATION AND COMPARABILITY
Membership-type counts reconcile to reported totals in all three years:
2022-23: 5424 + 1452 + 2442 = 9318.
2023-24: 5433 + 1467 + 2228 = 9128.
2024-25: 4969 + 1461 + 2018 = 8448.
Published membership-type shares in 2023-24 are 59, 16, 24 (sum 99).
These are preserved, not silently rounded or corrected. Calculated shares are
provided independently for percentage visualisations and sum to approximately 100.

2022-23 PARC gender shares are 29% male and 70% female. The report uses the
heading Demographics under PARC Members, without specifying a membership subtype.
The 1-point remainder is not assigned to any invented category.
2023-24 explicitly labels gender counts as Full Access members: Male 3757,
Female 1467, Non-binary 3. These sum to 5227, not the Full Access total of 5433.
The drawn bar proportions also disagree with the printed values. All values and
labels are preserved; do not swap gender labels or force a reconciliation.
2024-25 Full Access gender shares are 40%, 59%, 1%. Counts are not supplied.
Avoid a three-year gender trend without clarification from the publisher.

Membership type shows service choice; gender composition describes members.
Neither alone measures disability inclusion, financial barriers or health outcomes.
