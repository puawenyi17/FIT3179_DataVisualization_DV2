Includes all 12 overseas country-labelled rows (107 listed athletes).
Excludes Australia Non-Masters, all named swimming clubs (including Swim Fort Lauderdale),
and Composite Relay Teams. This is not a census of overseas participants.
Country labels and team codes are retained exactly as published. Counts use Athletes,
not Entries (one athlete may enter multiple events). Female + Male = Athletes in each row.
Rows are ordered by athlete count descending, then team label alphabetically for ties.

Map links represent team affiliation with the competition, not recorded journeys,
residential origins or measured routes. Country points are manually selected approximate
cartographic anchors; destination is an approximate Southport venue position.
They must not be used to calculate travel distances or emissions.
Line width is linear in athletes (0.4 SVG pixels per athlete), fixed across filters.
All country labels and counts are available in a visible HTML table beside the map.
